Mix Your Music. Simply.

Multitrack audio editing for iPhone.

MIXIVO helps you arrange, record and edit audio on a timeline. Combine tracks, trim and split clips, adjust volume and speed, and export your mix.

Your projects stay on your device

Audio projects, recordings and exported mixes are stored locally. Signing in does not upload your projects to the account service or create a cloud backup.

Optional account

You can use MIXIVO in FREE mode without an account. Email, Apple and Google sign-in provide access to your MIXIVO account. Account services use Supabase; Google sign-in requests only basic identity information (openid, email and profile), not Gmail, Drive or audio files.

For details on information handling, read the Privacy Policy. For help with MIXIVO or your account, contact mixivo.app@gmail.com.

Il tuo editor audio multitraccia per iPhone

MIXIVO permette di registrare, organizzare e modificare audio su una timeline: combina tracce, taglia e dividi clip, regola volume e velocità ed esporta il mix.

Progetti, registrazioni e mix esportati restano sul dispositivo. L’accesso all’account non carica i progetti online e non crea un backup cloud.

Puoi usare MIXIVO in modalità FREE senza account. Puoi accedere al tuo account con email, Apple o Google. L’accesso Google richiede soltanto le informazioni di identità di base, non Gmail, Drive o file audio.
